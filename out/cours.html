Media design: Overview

Start Date: 13/12/2024End Date: 25/12/2024Status: past

This course provides an overview of key concepts in media design, including typography, interaction design, visual storytelling, and emerging digital platforms. Students will engage in hands-on projects and critiques to explore creative and technical skills.

Covered topics:

  • Typography and layout
  • Interaction design principles
  • Visual storytelling
  • Digital prototyping
